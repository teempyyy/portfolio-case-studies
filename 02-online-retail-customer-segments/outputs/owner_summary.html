What to do

  1. Protect the top 33%. Champions and Loyal customers bring 75% of revenue. Give them priority service, early access to new ranges and volume pricing. Each Champion spent £6,639 on average last year.
  2. Win back 652 drifting customers before the next peak. "At risk" and "Can't lose" customers spent £794,673 last year but haven't ordered for 4 to 5 months on average. Contact the 57 "Can't lose" accounts personally; send the rest a win-back offer.
  3. Get new customers to a second order. Only 20% of new customers buy again the next month and 15% are still buying a year later (median of 11 monthly groups). 36% of last year's customers ordered once. A welcome series that aims for a second order within 30 days costs little and targets the biggest gap.
